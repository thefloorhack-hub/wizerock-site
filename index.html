One company, one simple idea.

Home projects are hard to plan. Prices are unclear, good contractors are hard to find, and materials come from many places.

Wizerock builds online platforms that make each step easier: an estimate you can trust, materials in one place, and contractors who compete for the job so homeowners do not have to chase quotes.

What we do

The services that run inside our brands today.

Estimates
Homeowners describe their project with a photo and sizes and get an estimate before anyone visits.
Private contractor bidding
Vetted contractors and suppliers bid on each project. Names and contact details stay private until both sides sign.
Materials
Natural stone pavers, marble, travertine, porcelain and ledger stone in one online shop.
Delivery network
A load board that connects contractors and suppliers with drivers for local deliveries.
Contractor marketplace
Contractors and suppliers post what they have or need and receive offers.
Guides
Plain-language articles on materials, installation and care for South Florida homes.

How a FloorHack project works

From the first photo to the signed job.

  1. Describe the project

    Add a photo, a short description and rough sizes.

  2. Get an estimate

    See a first price range, then book a site visit.

  3. Contractors bid

    Vetted contractors bid privately on the detailed job.

  4. Sign and start

    Accept one price. Details are shared once both sides sign.
